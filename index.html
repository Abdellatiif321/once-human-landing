What is Once Human?

Once Human is a multiplayer open-world survival game set in a post-apocalyptic future. You are a Meta-Human, one of the few who can withstand the Stardust contamination that has consumed the world. Survive together, build your territory, and uncover the truth behind the cosmic horror.
